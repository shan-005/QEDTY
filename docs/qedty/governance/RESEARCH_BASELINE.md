# Governance research baseline

The implementation follows the principle that governance should be continuous, documented, and connected to measurement and evidence. NIST AI RMF's Govern/Map/Measure/Manage structure is used as a conceptual organizing scheme, while ISO/IEC 42001 provides a management-system orientation. OECD AI Principles add a human-centric and trustworthy-AI framing.

The package does not assert legal compliance by implementing a few checks. Compliance is jurisdiction- and deployment-specific and must be assessed by qualified reviewers.
